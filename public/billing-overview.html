How the process works

01. Discovery
We define goal, audience, offer, must-have features and timeline.
02. Scope
You get a clear scope, price range, milestone plan and next steps.
03. Build
Design and development happen in visible milestones with feedback points.
04. Launch
Testing, final polish, deployment support and handoff notes.

Payment terms

  • Smaller projects: usually 50% upfront and 50% before launch/handoff.
  • Larger projects: usually 30% start, 40% build milestone, 30% before launch.
  • Urgent timelines, unclear scope or heavy content work can affect the final quote.
  • Hosting, domains, paid tools, third-party subscriptions and payment provider fees are billed separately.

Fair-price principle: the goal is not to charge for every tiny change, but to keep the scope honest. Small polish is included; new features, new flows or major direction changes are estimated separately before work starts.
